# Paid Ads Assessment

## Recommendation

Do not run paid ads during the free controlled pilot.

## Why

Paid acquisition is poorly matched to the current stage:

- developer audiences usually ignore generic ads;
- crypto-related ads face platform restrictions and review friction;
- the product is API-key gated and manually reviewed, so broad traffic is not
  the bottleneck;
- there is no public pricing or self-serve checkout to optimize against;
- the current goal is high-quality feedback, not raw signups;
- CAC would likely exceed the value of unqualified pilot requests.

## Better Near-Term Channels

Prioritize:

1. MCP directories and registries.
2. Public integration guides.
3. Build-in-public technical posts.
4. Direct outreach to agent/Web3 builders.
5. Targeted conversations in MCP and DeFi automation communities.

## When Ads Might Make Sense

Revisit small paid tests only after Phase 4 monetization begins and these are
true:

- stable public MCP endpoint;
- clear conversion event beyond form submission;
- proof that pilot users return after the first call;
- enough usage data to identify buyer segments;
- clear paid offer or enterprise/design-partner motion;
- no unresolved public-claim blockers.

## Possible Future Tests

If Phase 4 conditions are met, consider:

- small Google Search test for exact-intent queries;
- sponsorship in a technical AI-agent newsletter;
- retargeting to visitors who read integration docs.

Keep budget small and stop quickly if leads are not technically qualified.
